---
title: "Two Books Recommendations for Getting Started With Economics"
date: 2020-03-18
draft: true
---

Naked Economics by Charles Wheelan

Undercover Economist by Tim Harford

Both books offer a "qualitative" introduction to economic concepts: no
equations, formulas, or scary math. Both answer fundamental questions about
human behavior using tools of economics and provide a framework for answering
other questions using economics.
